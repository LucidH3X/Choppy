Split big CSV files into pieces that fit under an upload limit. Every piece keeps the header row, and no row gets cut in half. It all runs in your browser, so your files never leave your computer.

    Download zip again

    Files that are already small enough go into the zip unchanged.

    If your files have more than one header line (like a row of column names plus a row of descriptions), set Header rows to match so every piece gets all of them.

    A single row bigger than the limit can't be split, so it gets its own piece and is flagged.
